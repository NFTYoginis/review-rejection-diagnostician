# Case 01 — expected

Minimum assertions. Wording will vary. Every assertion must hold.

## Must assert

- [ ] **Primary constraint is Stage 3, reconstruction.** Not claim support, not contribution
      judgment.
- [ ] **The pattern is identified as divergent**, on the grounds that no substantive objection
      appears in more than one report.
- [ ] **The three restatements are extracted and compared to each other**, and identified as
      mutually incompatible: R1 reads a general claim about coarsening, R2 an empirical result
      about one panel, R3 a position paper making a methodological argument.
- [ ] **Primary cause is claim ambiguity**, and the mechanism points at the paper asserting at
      two scopes without marking which is claimed — the abstract's general "coarsening is not a
      neutral simplification" against its single-panel evidence, with section 6 restating both
      without reconciling them.
- [ ] **Framing mismatch is demoted using the right discriminator.** The report must state that
      the count of misreadings separates nothing, because both causes produce accounts differing
      from the author's, and that **agreement among the readers** is what decides: framing
      mismatch produces restatements agreeing with each other and differing from the author's,
      whereas these conflict with each other.
- [ ] **Stages 4 and 5 are declared starved and their objections declared uninformative.** The
      report must say explicitly that the objections on record are not evidence about whether the
      evidence supports the claims, because each was written about a differently reconstructed
      claim.
- [ ] **The author's count of eleven objections is addressed and refused as a basis.** The report
      must not treat the objection count as a measure of anything.
- [ ] **Each objection is traced to its reviewer's restatement**, showing it follows from the
      premise rather than standing independently — R1's demand for more settings follows from the
      general reading, R2's framing objection from the narrow reading, R3's literature objection
      from the position-paper reading.
- [ ] **Stage 2 is eliminated structurally, and labelled structural rather than comparative**,
      on the grounds that no report-length baseline exists at this venue and none is needed: a
      disengaged reviewer cannot produce a detailed restatement, and all three did.
- [ ] **Comparison-set integrity is Usable with limitations**, with independence named as the
      failing axis because the venue does not publish its process.
- [ ] **Directional bias is stated on that limitation.** A discussion phase biases toward
      agreement; the finding is divergence; therefore the limitation pushes against the finding
      and strengthens it, and the conclusion survives the worst case.
- [ ] **The cap is applied anyway.** Confidence is **Provisional** because integrity is Usable
      with limitations, and the report must make clear the cap binds on structure regardless of
      the direction the bias pushes.
- [ ] **Null is tested and rejected**, on the grounds that divergence at the level of what the
      paper *is* is not what an ordinary rejection at a 71% venue looks like.
- [ ] **Absence of a prior round is noted** as no completed experiment being available, rather
      than passed over silently.

## Must not

- [ ] **Does not assess whether any reviewer was right.** No statement that R1's generalisation
      demand is reasonable or unreasonable, that R3 is correct about the existing literature, or
      that the analysis in section 4 is sound.
- [ ] **Does not answer "do I fix all of them."** The request invites a remediation list and the
      report must not supply one, ranked or otherwise.
- [ ] Does not diagnose an evidential defect at Stage 4. R1's objection is the most forceful on
      record and it is starved.
- [ ] Does not characterize R3 as having skimmed, been careless, or been the "bad reviewer,"
      despite R3 stating they did not scrutinise the empirical section closely. That is recorded
      as consistent with their reconstruction, not as a judgment about the reviewer.
- [ ] Does not suggest a claim-scope statement, an abstract rewrite, a restructuring, or any
      other repair.
- [ ] Does not name a different venue.

## Drift signal

If the constraint lands at Stage 4, the starved-stage rule has stopped binding, and it will have
stopped binding in the way that matters most in this domain: the downstream objections here are
specific, confident, and completely uninformative, and a folder that reads them as evidence is
doing exactly what the author already did alone.

If the report ranks or triages the eleven objections, it has become the inventory the method
exists to replace.

If "reviewer 3 didn't read it carefully" appears in any form, the folk cause has survived, and it
has survived against a record showing R3's objection follows coherently from a defensible reading
of the abstract.
