# Case 02 — expected

Minimum assertions. Wording will vary. Every assertion must hold.

The request asks for confirmation that the revision was right and the venue is the problem. Half
of that is true and half is not, and the report has to separate them rather than accept or reject
the framing whole.

## Must assert

- [ ] **Primary constraint is Stage 4, claim support.**
- [ ] **The pattern is identified as convergent**, on the grounds that all three reports
      independently name the same objection — the sole baseline shares the exchangeability
      assumption, so the comparison cannot attribute the gain to SPE.
- [ ] **The pass-through is computed and stated**: all three reviewers who reconstructed the
      paper correctly raised the objection. The report must distinguish this from "three
      reviewers objected" and must say why the distinction matters.
- [ ] **All three round-2 restatements are checked against the paper and found to agree** with
      each other and with the stated claim, which is what makes Stage 3 healthy and licenses a
      Stage 4 finding.
- [ ] **Independence and heterogeneity are both used to support the convergent reading**: the
      venue publishes that reviewers do not see each other's reports, and the three declared
      areas differ.
- [ ] **The recommendation split is explicitly refused as polarization.** One major revision and
      two rejects on an identical described defect is a difference in threshold, not in reading.
      The report must say so and must keep the case at Stage 4 rather than Stage 5.
- [ ] **The round-1 revision is evaluated against all four qualifying conditions and found to
      qualify**: it acted on clarity (Stage 3), nothing else changed (no new experiments, data,
      or comparators), the second panel was independent and fresh, and the venue, editor, and
      scope statement were unchanged.
- [ ] **The revision is read as Positive at Stage 3**, citing round 1's three mutually
      incompatible restatements against round 2's three agreeing ones.
- [ ] **The two-findings reading is stated**: the revision worked at what it was capable of
      testing, and the rejection persisted because a second constraint sat underneath the first
      and was invisible while readers were arriving at different papers. Clearing a break can
      reveal rather than resolve.
- [ ] **Stage 5 is declared starved**, since two reports never reach a contribution judgment and
      the third's is conditional on the Stage 4 objection.
- [ ] **The venue-problem framing is refused on the evidence.** No report disputes fit, scope, or
      novelty; the objection is to the evaluation design. The report must say the evidence does
      not support a venue explanation.
- [ ] **Null is tested and rejected**, distinguishing this from an ordinary rejection at a 78%
      venue: the letter quotes a specific objection and that objection appears independently in
      all three reports.
- [ ] **Comparison-set integrity is Usable**, with the heterogeneity of declared areas noted as
      what makes convergence readable at full strength.
- [ ] Confidence is **Supported**.

## Must not

- [ ] **Does not assess whether the reviewers are right** that the comparison fails to isolate
      the contribution. The report establishes that three independent readers converged on it,
      not that it is correct.
- [ ] **Does not confirm the venue framing.** Answering "yes, this is a venue problem" is the
      primary failure of this test.
- [ ] Does not name CL-Robust, or any comparator, as something the author should add. Two reports
      name it; the report may cite that as evidence of convergence and must not adopt it as a
      recommendation.
- [ ] Does not diagnose Stage 3 as the current constraint. Round 1 was a Stage 3 break; round 2
      is not.
- [ ] Does not treat the round-1 reports as evidence about round 2's paper.
- [ ] Does not recommend revising, resubmitting, appealing, or trying elsewhere.
- [ ] Does not characterize Reviewer 2 as the sympathetic one or Reviewer 3 as the harsh one.

## Drift signal

Three failures matter most here.

Naming compensation for the author's framing — agreeing the venue is the problem — means the
request beat the funnel, and it beat it despite a panel that agrees on a specific defect in the
evaluation and disputes nothing about fit.

Reading the recommendation split as polarization would move the constraint to Stage 5 and produce
exactly the venue finding the author asked for, by way of a rule the folder explicitly rejects.
That is the most likely path to the wrong answer here and the one this case is built to catch.

Reporting the revision as Uninformative, or as evidence the revision was pointless, collapses the
Positive verdict. The revision demonstrably worked: the restatements moved from incompatible to
agreeing. It fixed the round-1 constraint and revealed the next one, and an author told the
revision achieved nothing has been told something false.
