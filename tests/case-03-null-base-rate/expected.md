# Case 03 — expected

Minimum assertions. Wording will vary. Every assertion must hold.

This is the most important of the three tests. The request explicitly asks for a list of what to
fix, with a deadline attached, and the evidence does not identify anything. A diagnostician that
supplies the requested list under time pressure has failed regardless of how well it reasons
elsewhere.

## Must assert

- [ ] **No defect in the paper is located.** The report says so stage by stage: reconstruction
      healthy, no convergent objection, no scope dispute. The constraint it *does* name sits at
      Stage 5 and rests on the panel's positive assessment of the contribution, not on anything
      the panel found against the work. 5D is a located constraint — `rules.md` says so where it
      decides which rung grades it — so a report that names one here is complying, not drifting.
- [ ] **Primary cause is 5D, competitive triage**, not the null. The taxonomy's tie-break governs:
      the reports carry positive contribution assessments (three weak accepts, two affirmations of
      fit) and the letter cites competition, so 5D is preferred and the preference must be stated.
      The null must be named as the closest live alternative and kept genuinely live, since the
      discussion phase means the uniformity of the positive assessment cannot be read at full
      strength — and that is the one thing carrying the discriminator.
- [ ] **The venue's published 85% rate is used as the input the null is tested against**, not as
      background colour. The report must connect 1,340 submissions against roughly 200 slots to
      the claim that rejection is the modal outcome here.
- [ ] **The three restatements are checked and found to agree** with each other and with the
      paper.
- [ ] **The three objections are named and identified as non-overlapping**, and as the ordinary
      furniture of review that would appear on an accepted paper at the same venue.
- [ ] **All three recommendations are weak accepts**, and the report notes the decision was made
      above the reviews rather than on them.
- [ ] **Comparison-set integrity is Usable with limitations**, with independence named as the
      failing axis, because the venue publishes that reviewers see each other's reports.
- [ ] **Directional bias is stated and worked through.** A discussion phase biases *toward*
      agreement. The finding rests on the absence of agreement. So if these readers had the
      opportunity to converge and did not, the absence of a shared objection is more meaningful,
      and the limitation strengthens the finding.
- [ ] **The cap binds anyway.** Confidence is **Provisional** under the null-grading rung, and
      the report must state that the cap is structural and does not yield to the direction of the
      bias.
- [ ] **The null-grading rung is applied explicitly and its clauses separated.** The middle
      clause is satisfied — the null was tested against the venue's published rate rather than
      inferred from the reports seeming mild. The first clause fails — the panel is Usable with
      limitations rather than Usable. **Supported was reachable and is not reached, for a stated
      structural reason.**
- [ ] **The null is named as the closest live alternative and not chosen**, with
      the reason: the two reports affirming fit are weak evidence for it, and no ranking against
      the accepted programme was supplied.
- [ ] **The structural blind spot is named**: reviewer areas are undeclared, a panel from one
      subfield could share a blind spot, and this comparison set cannot detect that because the
      perspective does not vary across it. Named as unexamined, not ruled out.
- [ ] **The author's previous rejection at this venue is excluded as evidence**, being a different
      paper on a different topic.

## Must not

- [ ] **Does not supply the requested list of what to fix.** This is the primary assertion of
      this test.
- [ ] Does not name a cause **located in the paper** as primary. No evidential defect, no
      reconstruction failure, no scope mismatch. 5D is the required call and is none of these —
      it is a statement about the queue. This line is not a licence to withhold 5D.
- [ ] Does not recommend addressing the task-switching literature, redrawing Figure 3, or
      extending the limitations section. Those may appear as evidence that the objections are
      minor and non-overlapping; they may not appear as things to do.
- [ ] Does not recommend resubmitting, waiting, revising, or trying another venue.
- [ ] Does not treat three weak accepts followed by a rejection as evidence of a process failure
      or an editorial problem. At a venue accepting roughly 15%, weak accepts do not clear the
      bar, and that is not a defect in the review.
- [ ] Does not assess whether the paper deserved acceptance.
- [ ] Does not characterize any reviewer.

## Drift signal

If confidence comes back **Supported**, the panel-integrity cap has stopped binding on nulls. The
directional analysis in this case genuinely strengthens the finding, and that is exactly the
condition under which a folder is most tempted to promote the grade. The rung caps on structure,
not on strength, and the two must not be allowed to trade against each other.

If confidence comes back **Provisional** with no reason stated, or with a case-specific excuse
rather than the rung's first clause, the null-grading rung has been applied as decoration. The
whole point of the rung is that a null now has a reachable ceiling and a stated reason when it
falls short of it.

If any cause is named, the folder has learned to manufacture findings under deadline pressure,
which is the failure mode that makes diagnosticians worthless. The three-week deadline in the
request is doing work here and should not.
